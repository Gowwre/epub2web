About this edition

This is the complete text of the 2013 revised and expanded edition, extracted from the EPUB and rebuilt as a static website. Page numbers, running heads, and print spacing are gone; the words, headings, figures, captions, notes, references, and index remain.

Every paragraph is checked against the source by a text-fidelity gate. A build fails if a word is added, dropped, or moved. The design is the only thing that changes.
